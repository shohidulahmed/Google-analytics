The Stack Burger Story

Burgers have become one of the world's favourite comfort foods. At Stack Burger Co., we wanted to create a menu that combines classic flavours with a few modern twists.

Choosing the Right Burger

Whether you prefer a classic cheeseburger, a double bacon burger, crispy chicken or a veggie option, there's something for everyone.

Our menu is designed to make choosing your next burger easy. Start with your favourite patty and then add the toppings and sauces you enjoy.

Our Burgers

The Classic Stack is inspired by the traditional American cheeseburger. The Double Bacon Stack is designed for anyone looking for something bigger, while the Spicy Chicken Burger brings some extra heat.

Why We Love Burgers

Burgers are simple, customisable and easy to enjoy. They work just as well for a quick lunch as they do for a relaxed weekend meal with friends.
